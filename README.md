# arcade

Seven small browser games in one HTML file. No libraries, no build step. **Play: [thatonesharpdude.github.io/arcade](https://thatonesharpdude.github.io/arcade/)**

| # | game | the idea |
|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | make-a-market | Quote a bid and an ask on a dice or coin contract. An informed trader picks you off if you are on the wrong side of fair; customers pay your spread. Expected P&L is the skill, realized P&L adds the dice. |
| 2 | fair-price | Strip the margin out of a two-sided price. Scored against the power method, with the proportional answer shown next to it. |
| 3 | kelly-coin | A coin that lands heads 60% of the time, $25 and 30 flips. Size your bets and race a Kelly bettor on the same flips. |
| 4 | four-in-a-row | Against a minimax search with alpha-beta pruning, five plies deep. |
| 5 | tic-tac-toe | Against a full game-tree search. It never loses; a draw is the best result there is. |
| 6 | mines | Classic mine-sweeping. The first dig is always safe; flag mode for touch screens. |
| 7 | meet-day | Squat, bench, deadlift, three attempts each. Pick the weight, then time the drive. Heavier means a smaller window and a faster bar. |

Keys `1`-`7` switch games. Best scores are kept in your browser only.
