# arcade

Fourteen small browser games in one HTML file. No libraries, no build step. **Play: [thatonesharpdude.github.io/arcade](https://thatonesharpdude.github.io/arcade/)**

### trade
| game | the idea |
|:--|:--|
| make-a-market | Quote a bid and an ask on a dice or coin contract. An informed trader picks you off if you are on the wrong side of fair; customers pay your spread. Expected P&L is the skill, realized P&L adds the dice. |
| fair-price | Strip the margin out of a two-sided price. Scored against the power method, with the proportional answer shown next to it. |
| kelly-coin | A coin that lands heads 60% of the time, $25 and 30 flips. Size your bets and race a Kelly bettor on the same flips. |
| pot-odds | Call or fold a poker draw: exact equity vs the share of the pot you must put in, with the rule of 2 and 4 alongside. |
| card-counting | Hi-Lo running count as cards flash, then convert to a true count. |

### brain
| game | the idea |
|:--|:--|
| four-in-a-row | Against a minimax search with alpha-beta pruning, five plies deep. |
| tic-tac-toe | Against a full game-tree search. It never loses; a draw is the best result there is. |
| mines | Classic mine-sweeping. The first dig is always safe; flag mode for touch screens. |
| chess-vision | Blindfold drills: square colors and knight distances, 60 seconds each. |

### sport
| game | the idea |
|:--|:--|
| meet-day | Squat, bench, deadlift, three attempts each. Pick the weight, then time the drive. Heavier means a smaller window and a faster bar. |
| pr-chase | Pick a percent of your max and grind out reps against building fatigue. Estimates your max from the set. |
| shootout | Ten penalty shots against a goalie who learns your habits. Unpredictable wins. |
| hr-derby | Time your swing on fastballs, sliders and changeups. Ten outs. |
| free-throws | Two taps for aim and power; a 30-second buzzer mode that speeds up when you get hot. |

Keys `1`-`9` switch to the first nine games. Best scores are kept in your browser only.
